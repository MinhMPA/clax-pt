## CLASS-PT internals dumps (`*_internals.npz`)

Not produced by `scripts/generate_classpt_reference.py`, and NOT regenerated by
`scripts/write_classpt_manifest.py` (which rewrites everything above this
section) — this block is hand-maintained. These are C-level dumps of
`nonlinear_pt_loop()`'s own working arrays, taken by task D1 to isolate the
one-loop residual, and promoted here by task D2 so the IR-resummation unit test
(`tests/test_ir_resummation_classpt.py`) has a CLASS-PT oracle with no CLASS-PT
at test time. They are **not** campaign references: nothing in the campaign
accuracy suite reads them, and they carry no `bias_json` and none of the nine
assembled spectra.

Contents. Every array except `lnk_l`/`lnpk_l` is a `nonlinear_pt_loop()` local
on the **`kdisc` grid** (`Nmax = 256`, `kdisc[i] = 5e-5·h·e^{iΔ}`), in CLASS
units — `kdisc` in 1/Mpc, every `P*` in Mpc³ (multiply by `h³`, and divide
`kdisc` by `h`, for clax's (Mpc/h)³ / (h/Mpc) convention):

- `kdisc`, `Pdisc` — the FFTLog grid and the linear spectrum on it
  (`nonlinear_pt.c:3230-3243`: `exp` of the natural cubic spline of `lnpk_l`).
- `Pnw`, `Pw`, `Pbin`, `Ptree` — the IR-resummation outputs
  (`nonlinear_pt_ir_resummation()`, `:2979-2999`).
- `P13`, `P22`, `P1loop` — the real-space one-loop pieces.
- 12 `P22_mu*`/`P22_mu*_w`, 12 `P13_mu*`/`P13_mu*_w`, 6 `P13UV_mu*` — the 30 RSD
  loop channels and their UV counterterms, before the μ-integration.
- 9 `P1loop_{0,2,4}_{vv,vd,dd}`, 6 `Ptree_{0,2,4}_*`, 3 `P_CTR_*` — the assembled
  multipole rows.
- `lnk_l`, `lnpk_l` — the **132-point** linear table `ppt->k[scalars]`
  (`pnlpt->k_size`), `ln k` in 1/Mpc and `ln P` in Mpc³. This is the table
  CLASS-PT itself splines onto the DST grid at `:2782`; `Pdisc` is a 256-point
  re-sampling of the same spline, so it carries strictly less information.
- scalars (0-d float64) `h`, `fz`, `hratio`, `Dratio`, `z`, plus `pk_mult`,
  `pk_lin`, `pk_m_lin`, `k_h`, `params_json`, `scalars_json` copied from the
  matching campaign run for convenience.
- scalars (0-d float64) `sigmav`, `SigmaBAO`, `deltaSigmaBAO` — CLASS-PT's own
  `sigmav` (`nonlinear_pt.c:3265-3267`) and the two BAO damping scales returned
  by `nonlinear_pt_ir_resummation()` (`:2944-2947`), in CLASS units (Mpc², i.e.
  multiply by `h²` for clax's (Mpc/h)²). 79 keys per file.
